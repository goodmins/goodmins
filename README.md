## Goodmins

**Good habits earn good minutes.** A calm, private Android app that trades sleep, movement and reading for screen time. One point is one minute on the apps you want to use less.

- Website: https://goodmins.github.io
- Get it free on the PhonePe Appstore: https://www.phonepeappstore.com/apps/productivity/goodmins/app.goodmins/
- Privacy: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
- Contact: goodmins.app@gmail.com

Made in India.
